Our story

Jewellery made slowly, by one pair of hands

Morrow makes engagement rings one at a time, to order. Each ring is designed, modelled and finished with the same care, whether it comes from our collection or begins as an idea of your own.

The designer

I’m Tanin

I’ve spent around ten years designing and making gold jewellery. My work has taken me through every stage of production, from the first drawing and the CAD model to casting, setting and the final polish.

I studied industrial design, then luxury jewellery management, both to master’s level. Industrial design taught me how objects are built to last; luxury jewellery taught me what makes a piece feel precious.

Morrow brings the two together: rings that are beautiful to look at and properly made to be worn every day.

What we believe

The Morrow way

Made to order

Nothing sits in a drawer waiting to be sold. Your ring is made for you once we have agreed every detail.

Designer and maker

The person who designs your ring understands exactly how it will be made, so nothing is lost between drawing and finished piece.

Built to last

An engagement ring is worn every day for decades. Proportions, settings and band thickness are chosen with that in mind.
