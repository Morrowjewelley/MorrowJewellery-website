03

What you can choose

Every detail, your way

Metal: 18ct yellow, rose or white gold, or platinum.

Stone: diamonds, champagne diamonds, sapphires, morganite, emeralds and more, in the shape and size you want.

Setting: solitaire, three-stone, halo, bezel or something entirely new.

Details: Art Deco steps, pavé edges, split bands, or a hidden detail only you will know about.
